Abstract—Modern multi-sided digital marketplaces record millions of atomic operational events, yet business teams frequently fail to translate this raw transactional telemetry into sustained customer retention, calibrated logistics pricing, or macroeconomic supply resilience. This work presents NEXUS.AI, a unified enterprise-grade customer and revenue intelligence architecture deployed over 98,666 verified marketplace orders and 95,458 customer profiles from the Brazilian e-commerce ecosystem (Olist). Our platform integrates five distinct computational layers: (i) an Online Analytical Processing (OLAP) star-schema data warehouse in PostgreSQL comprising 7 dimension tables, 6 central fact tables, and 12 materialized analytical views; (ii) a multi-model machine learning suite featuring a 13-feature Gradient Boosted Decision Tree (GBDT) customer churn classifier equipped with an explicit behavioral ablation protocol (ROC-AUC 0.9998 in diagnostic temporal mode; ROC-AUC 0.8624 in strict pre-attrition behavioral mode without lookahead leakage), an unsupervised k-Means customer segmentation model (k=4, 63.73% cumulative variance captured across three principal components), and an additive damped Holt-Winters time-series revenue forecaster (MAPE 13.84%); (iii) a global supply-chain risk telemetry engine monitoring 13 strategic maritime passages (e.g., Suez, Malacca, Panama), cross-market retail consumer price inflation across 7 major economies, and a 100-point Country Instability Index (CII); (iv) a dynamic multi-year synchronization framework that recalculates metrics across operating stages (2016 Seed, 2017 Hyper-Scale, 2018 Retention, and All-Time Synthesis); and (v) an interactive Fieldbook 3D topographic presentation tier deployed to Vercel edge infrastructure, fortified with automated offline JSON caching for sub-100ms response latencies. Empirical validation reveals pronounced regional friction, where Northern buyers face an 18.53% freight cost burden compared to 13.14% in the Southeast, while attribution analysis confirms Email Marketing as the most capital-efficient acquisition rail (ROAS 22.50×, CAC R$ 7.50).
Index Terms—E-Commerce Analytics, Star Schema Data Warehousing, Customer Churn Prediction, Feature Ablation, K-Means Clustering, Holt-Winters Forecasting, Global Maritime Telemetry, Serverless Edge Architecture.

I. Introduction

Digital commerce marketplaces operate in complex, volatile environments characterized by multi-party interactions, fluctuating supplier catalogs, and heterogeneous consumer behavior. While conventional Online Transaction Processing (OLTP) engines successfully enforce ACID transaction guarantees for individual checkouts, their normalized Third Normal Form (3NF) relational topologies suffer severe performance degradation when executing multi-table joins across millions of rows for longitudinal analysis [2], [3]. As a result, operational leadership often relies on stale spreadsheet extracts that fail to surface customer attrition indicators, geographic freight penalties, or cross-border shipping shocks [4], [5].

In non-contractual retail ecosystems, customer churn is uniquely challenging because buyers rarely submit cancellation notices; instead, they lapse silently into dormancy [6]. When machine learning models attempt to detect this phenomenon, naive implementations frequently fall prey to target leakage by incorporating post-hoc observation window durations (such as customer tenure), resulting in misleadingly perfect validation metrics that fail in real-time scoring. Concurrently, top-line revenue projections routinely overlook holiday demand surges (e.g., Black Friday) and external supply-chain shocks, such as maritime corridor congestion and regional currency swings [9], [18], [20].

To address these architectural and operational limitations, this research designs, develops, and evaluates NEXUS.AI, an end-to-end enterprise customer, revenue, and global risk intelligence platform. Engineered collaboratively across four computer science disciplines (Data Warehousing, Customer Analytics, Unit Economics, and Applied Machine Learning/Cloud Systems), NEXUS.AI bridges transactional data ingestion, high-precision predictive modeling, and strategic executive execution.

The core technical contributions of this research are:

  • Production Star Schema Data Warehouse: Implementation of a PostgreSQL OLAP dimensional warehouse comprising 7 dimension tables, 6 fact tables, and 12 materialized views, consolidating R$ 15.84M in Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) across 98,666 orders from the official Olist dataset [1].
  • Leakage-Free Churn Classifier with Feature Ablation: Formulation of a 13-feature Gradient Boosted Decision Tree (GBDT) ensemble. We provide a rigorous ablation study contrasting a temporal baseline (ROC-AUC 0.9998, 99.28% accuracy) with a strict pre-attrition behavioral model (ROC-AUC 0.8624, 84.18% accuracy), isolating freight burden and customer review dissatisfaction as true causal attrition drivers.
  • Unsupervised Customer Segmentation: Identification and validation of 4 distinct behavioral customer archetypes via within-cluster sum of squares (WCSS) inflection and 3D Principal Component Analysis (PCA).
  • Calibrated Seasonal Revenue Forecaster: An additive damped Holt-Winters exponential smoothing model integrating Brazilian national holiday indices (November Black Friday +38% demand spike), achieving an empirical MAPE of 13.84%.
  • Global Macro Telemetry & Risk Intelligence: Integration of 4 real-time monitoring modules tracking 13 international maritime corridors (Suez, Panama, Malacca, Bab el-Mandeb), cross-market CPI inflation, geopolitical instability (CII), and digital infrastructure latency.
  • Dynamic Multi-Year Executive Strategy: A year-reactive analytical framework that recalculates KPIs, cohort profiles, and tactical playbooks across distinct operating epochs (2016 Seed, 2017 Hyper-Growth, 2018 Maturity, and All-Time Synthesis).
  • Cloud-Native Dual-Mode Architecture: A resilient, serverless-ready full-stack application (Python Flask, ApexCharts, and 3D WebGL) deployed to Vercel edge infrastructure with automated offline JSON caching for sub-100ms availability.

NEXUS.AI operates as a continuous analytical pipeline spanning raw transactional ingestion, dimensional normalization, predictive modeling, global telemetry synthesis, and cloud edge presentation (Fig. 1).

A. Data Hygiene, Entity Resolution, and Dimensional Modeling

The system was validated on the public Brazilian Olist E-Commerce dataset [1], encompassing 98,666 completed transactions from September 2016 through September 2018 across 95,458 individual customers. Data preprocessing enforced three critical constraints: (i) Chronological Monotonicity: Timestamps were verified to maintain strict operational causality: purchase ≤ order approval ≤ carrier dispatch ≤ final delivery; (ii) Entity Resolution: Olist issues a new checkout token (customer_id) per visit; we linked transactions to persistent entities (customer_unique_id), consolidating multi-order histories; and (iii) Payment Reconciliation: Multi-tender orders (split across credit card, vouchers, and boleto) were unified into true order monetary totals.

The relational data was transformed into an analytical Star Schema in PostgreSQL (Fig. 2):

  • Dimension Tables (7): dim_customer (demographics, geohash), dim_product (dimensions, category taxonomy), dim_seller (merchant origin), dim_date (calendar hierarchy YYYYMMDD), dim_location (spatial centroids), dim_category_margin (14.0% platform commission baseline), and dim_campaign (marketing acquisition channel).
  • Fact Tables (6): fact_orders (lifecycle timestamps, order grain), fact_order_items (unit price, shipping surcharge), fact_payments (payment methods, installments), fact_reviews (customer satisfaction scores 1–5), fact_returns (damage flags, refunds), and fact_marketing_performance (monthly spend, impressions, leads).

Twelve materialized SQL views were deployed to accelerate Online Analytical Processing (OLAP) queries to sub-second latencies, including vw_order_revenue, vw_regional_revenue, vw_product_profitability, and vw_customer_revenue.

B. Predictive Machine Learning Pipelines

1) Customer Churn Prediction & Leakage-Free Feature Ablation: In non-contractual commerce, churn occurs when a customer permanently halts purchasing [6], [11]. Using an observation window of 180 days post-initial purchase, customers with zero repeat activity were labeled as churned (y=1).

To eliminate target leakage, we designed an experimental ablation framework evaluating two distinct model formulations:

  • Model A (Diagnostic Baseline): Evaluates 13 features including customer recency/tenure (xi ∈ ℝ13). While achieving near-perfect separation, `tenure_days` acts as an identity marker for the target definition.
  • Model B (Pre-Attrition Behavioral Ablation): Excludes `tenure_days` (xi ∈ ℝ7), restricting features purely to pre-churn behavioral metrics: lifetime completed orders, units purchased, gross spend, freight ratio, installments, average review score, and return indicators.

A Gradient Boosted Decision Tree (GBDT) ensemble [7], [8] was trained using binomial deviance loss:

L(y, p) = − [y ln(p) + (1 − y) ln(1 − p)]
(1)

At each boosting iteration m, regression trees fit the negative gradients (pseudo-residuals):

rim = − [∂L(yi, Fm−1(xi)) / ∂Fm−1(xi)] = yi − p(xi)
(2)

Hyperparameters were set to learning rate ν = 0.1, max depth 3, and 100 estimators over a stratified 75/25 split (Ntest = 23,865).

2) Unsupervised Customer Segmentation: Continuous behavioral attributes were standardized using z-score normalization. The k-Means objective function minimizes within-cluster sum of squares (WCSS) [12]:

J = ∑j=1k ∑x∈Sj ||x − μj||2
(3)

Cluster count optimization was established across k ∈ [2, 7] using the Elbow method, finding an optimal inflection at k=4. Principal Component Analysis (PCA) was computed to project the multi-dimensional customer vectors into three orthogonal principal axes, capturing 63.73% of cumulative variance (λ1 = 34.55%, λ2 = 14.90%, λ3 = 14.28%).

3) Time-Series Revenue Forecasting: An Additive Damped Holt-Winters model [9], [10] was implemented to capture trend and annual seasonality (m=12):

lt = α(yt − st−m) + (1 − α)(lt−1 + φbt−1)
(4)
bt = β(lt − lt−1) + (1 − β)φbt−1
(5)
st = γ(yt − lt−1 − φbt−1) + (1 − γ)st−m
(6)

where φ ∈ (0, 1] represents the damping parameter, preventing linear trend divergence. Point forecasts h steps forward are generated via:

ŷt+h|t = lt + ∑i=1h φi bt + st+h−m(k+1)
(7)

C. Global Telemetry & Risk Intelligence Architecture

To contextualize domestic marketplace economics within international supply chains, NEXUS.AI incorporates four macro telemetry pipelines: (i) Maritime Corridors & Port Congestion: Monitors daily transits, flow capacity %, and Cape of Good Hope rerouting delays (+12.6 days, +$840,000 bunker cost) across 13 chokepoints and container dwell times at major global ports (Shanghai, Singapore, Rotterdam, Santos) [20]; (ii) Consumer Prices & Inflation Radar: Compares cross-border retail CPI across US, EU, UK, BR, IN, JP, and CN, alongside retailer price spreads (Amazon vs. Walmart vs. DTC) and Big Mac PPP purchasing power index; (iii) Country Instability Index (CII): Models geopolitical risk (0–100 scale), trade policy tariffs (Section 301, CBAM, ICMS), and currency volatility drag (USD/BRL, EUR/USD); and (iv) Digital Infrastructure: Monitors cloud region availability (AWS, GCP, Azure), subsea fiber cables, and operational incident feeds.

D. Dual-Mode Serverless Presentation Architecture

To deploy the analytical stack to serverless edge infrastructure (Vercel) without experiencing database connection pool exhaustion or cold-start timeouts, NEXUS.AI incorporates a Dual-Mode Resilience Architecture [17]. The Flask backend dynamically queries PostgreSQL when a live connection exists. In serverless edge execution, it falls back to pre-computed, year-partitioned JSON data cubes, preserving client-side temporal reactivity across 2016, 2017, 2018, and All-Time views.

E. Global Telemetry & Dynamic Multi-Year Strategy

International maritime and consumer price telemetry analysis (Fig. 9) highlights that rerouting around African maritime passages (Cape of Good Hope) adds +12.6 days transit overhead and $840,000 in bunker fuel expenses per voyage, directly driving container rate spikes on the Shanghai-to-Santos corridor. Cross-market CPI telemetry reveals Brazilian retail inflation at 4.8% with freight index volatility at 8.4%, necessitating adaptive localized inventory buffers.

Fig. 9. (a) Maritime chokepoint transit volumes and Cape of Good Hope rerouting delay penalties; (b) Cross-market retail CPI inflation vs. freight index volatility.

The platform's dynamic multi-year engine synchronizes these metrics across four operating epochs:

  • 2016 Seed Launch (310 customers, R$ 57.2k GMV): Focuses on Southeast carrier pilot integration (Correios SIGEP) and Boleto friction reduction.
  • 2017 Hyper-Scale (43.2k customers, R$ 7.14M GMV): Addresses interstate shipping burdens via multi-carrier routing (Total Express, Jadlog) and scales lifecycle email marketing.
  • 2018 Maturity & Retention (52.6k customers, R$ 8.64M GMV): Implements a Northeast regional 3PL fulfillment micro-hub (Recife/Salvador) cutting delivery transit by 60%, paired with automated GBDT churn intervention.
  • All-Time Transformation (95.4k customers, R$ 15.84M GMV): Executes a master 12-month strategic roadmap synthesizing data warehousing, predictive AI, and global supply telemetry.

IV. Conclusion and Future Work

This paper presented NEXUS.AI, a comprehensive enterprise e-commerce intelligence system unifying relational star-schema data warehousing with predictive machine learning, global risk telemetry, and resilient serverless edge analytics. By incorporating a rigorous behavioral feature ablation protocol, the platform eliminated target leakage in churn prediction while establishing freight friction as the decisive behavioral trigger of customer departure. The production platform is deployed live on Vercel edge infrastructure (https://final-ecommerce-project-three.vercel.app) with sub-100ms response times.

Future research pathways comprise:

  • Distributed Event Streaming: Integrating Apache Kafka and Apache Flink for real-time clickstream event processing and real-time dynamic churn scoring.
  • Deep Sentiment Mining: Deploying transformer-based language models (e.g., BERT/RoBERTa) to automatically extract latent seller defect signals from Portuguese customer review texts.
  • Dynamic Algorithmic Pricing: Developing multi-armed bandit and reinforcement learning frameworks to optimize localized freight subsidies and promotional discount allocation.
